Permissions Explained

A plain-language guide to the Android permissions used by the current MesTok MVP.

Notification Access

Why it is requested: to detect supported TikTok notifications and read the visible title and message preview supplied by Android. The app stores the latest preview locally on the device. It cannot read messages that are not exposed through notifications.

Display over other apps

Why it is requested: to display the small floating MesTok bubble over other apps after a supported TikTok notification arrives. The bubble currently shows the M mark, not the notification text.

Do I have to grant these permissions?

No. They are optional. You can use Android settings to grant or revoke them. Features depending on a permission will not work while it is disabled.

How to change permissions

Open MesTok → Settings, or use Android Settings → Apps → Special app access. Menu names may vary by phone manufacturer and Android version.

What MesTok does not request

The current MVP does not request your TikTok password or use a permission to bypass TikTok's security. Official TikTok sign-in and any future permissions will be separately explained before activation.
