Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. This policy describes the current experimental Android version of MesTok (version 0.1.0). It will be updated before any feature changes the data we process.

In short: MesTok currently has no user accounts, does not operate a MesTok messaging server, and does not upload notification text to MesTok servers. The latest TikTok notification preview is stored locally on your Android device.

1. Who operates MesTok

MesTok is an independent companion application project. It is not owned, sponsored, endorsed, or operated by TikTok. TikTok is a separate service governed by its own policies.

2. Information processed by the current app

  • Notification preview: If you explicitly enable Android Notification Access, MesTok reads notifications posted by supported TikTok packages and extracts the notification title and visible text preview, when Android makes them available.
  • Local storage: The latest title, message preview, and notification timestamp are saved in the app's private local preferences on your device.
  • Permissions state: The app checks whether you enabled notification access and display-over-other-apps permission so it can show their status in Settings.

3. What the current app does not collect

The current MVP does not require registration, does not ask for your TikTok password, does not run a MesTok account database, and does not send notification previews to a MesTok server. The current code does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Why notification access is needed

Notification access is used only to detect notifications from supported TikTok app package names and read the preview Android exposes. MesTok cannot use this permission to retrieve every TikTok direct message, hidden conversation, deleted message, or message that TikTok/Android does not expose in a notification.

5. Floating overlay permission

If you separately grant the Android “display over other apps” permission, MesTok can show a small floating M bubble when a supported TikTok notification arrives. The bubble currently displays the MesTok mark; it does not display the notification text. You can revoke this permission at any time in Android settings.

6. Sharing and third parties

MesTok does not currently transmit notification content to us or share it with advertisers. TikTok and Android remain separate services and may process information under their own privacy terms. If future MesTok versions add cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other third-party services, this policy will be revised before those features are enabled.

7. Retention and deletion

The current app retains only the latest notification preview in local app storage, until it is replaced, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. You can revoke notification access at any time. For instructions, see Data Deletion.

8. Security

Local app-private storage is used for the preview. No storage method can guarantee absolute security; protect your device with a screen lock and avoid sharing notification contents you consider sensitive.

9. Children

MesTok is not designed specifically for children. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors and review the permissions granted on the device.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change as the app develops. The effective date appears above. For questions or privacy requests, use the MesTok Support page.
